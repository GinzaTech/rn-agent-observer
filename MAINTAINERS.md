The project currently has one active maintainer. This is disclosed intentionally:
CODEOWNERS and automated checks clarify ownership, but they do not remove the
bus-factor risk. Independent approval will become a required branch rule only after
a second active reviewer can satisfy it without deadlocking routine maintenance.

## Becoming a reviewer or maintainer

Reviewers are nominated after several constructive contributions across at least
two pull requests or equivalent issue/fixture work. Expected signals include:

- preserving evidence availability and active-action safety boundaries;
- writing deterministic tests and reporting runtime limitations honestly;
- respectful issue triage and useful review of another contributor's work;
- familiarity with one maintained area such as schemas, Android evidence,
  security/privacy, release engineering, instrumentation or documentation.

Maintainer promotion follows `GOVERNANCE.md`: public rationale, nominee consent and
no unresolved maintainer objection. Repository and npm access are granted with
least privilege. Release access additionally requires hardware-backed account
security and a successful supervised release.

Emeritus and conflict-of-interest rules are defined in `GOVERNANCE.md`.
