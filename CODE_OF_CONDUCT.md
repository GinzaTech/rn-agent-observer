# Community Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, safe, and constructive community for everyone,
regardless of experience, identity, background, disability, appearance, or point
of view. Participation in this project means treating other people with respect
and helping maintain a technically rigorous environment without hostility.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, specific, and patient in technical discussion.
- Critique ideas and evidence, not people.
- Give and accept actionable feedback in good faith.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's data or communications.
- Disclose conflicts of interest and correct mistakes promptly.
- Keep security research authorized, scoped, and responsible.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, stalking, intimidation, or discriminatory language.
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Trolling, sustained disruption, personal attacks, or deliberate humiliation.
- Publishing private information, credentials, or unredacted runtime artifacts
  without explicit permission.
- Encouraging unauthorized access, destructive testing, or harm to third-party
  applications, devices, services, or people.
- Retaliating against someone who raises a good-faith concern.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions,
release spaces, community events, and private project communication. It also
applies when someone officially represents the project in another public space.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainers through the private
contact method listed on the [GinzaTech GitHub organization](https://github.com/GinzaTech).
If no private channel is available, ask a maintainer to establish one without
including sensitive details in a public issue. Security vulnerabilities must use
the separate process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Reports should include what happened, where and when it happened, relevant context,
and any immediate safety concern. Maintainers will limit disclosure to the people
needed to review and resolve the report. Conflicts of interest will be recused.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, issue a private warning, impose a temporary
participation restriction, or permanently remove a participant when necessary.
The response will consider impact, pattern, intent, repair, and community safety.
Knowingly false reports or retaliation may themselves violate this policy, but an
unsubstantiated good-faith report will not.

This policy is inspired by the
[Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
